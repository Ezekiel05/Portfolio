QR Attendance Pro

Employee attendance with QR badges and a kiosk scanner

How a project with me goes

Small steps, working software early, and no surprises on scope.

  1. Talk it through

    You describe what's slowing you down. I ask questions until I understand the real job the software has to do.

  2. See a prototype

    Within days you get something clickable, so we can fix the plan before the expensive part.

  3. Build in rounds

    I ship working pieces, you try them, and we adjust. You always know what's done and what's next.

  4. Launch and hand over

    I deploy it, show you how to use it, and leave you with the code and simple notes for maintaining it.

An engineer who writes software
